Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Beach Cocktails Merge mobile game ("the game", "we") uses on Android and iOS. Beach Cocktails Merge is published by the developer listed on its Google Play and App Store pages. Contact: sekiphayit1982@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There is no account and no login. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • We run no server of our own. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • The game can show optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier, with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, coins, gems, energy, boosters, daily rewards, settings and tutorial progress on your device. This data never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. It is not backed up to any server of ours; your device's own backup feature may include it, under Google's or Apple's terms.

2. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs) are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. Your payment details are handled only by Google or Apple; we never see your card or account details. The game receives only the purchase confirmation it needs to deliver the item. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob: you choose to watch one, for example to refill energy or to claim a daily reward. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads software.

Consent. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but are not personalised. On iOS the game asks for tracking permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared.

Your choices. Rewarded videos are always optional. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Children

Beach Cocktails Merge is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob, Google Play) and Apple as described above, so that ads and purchases work.

6. Security and retention

Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google or Apple is kept under their own policies. Their connections are encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data on any server, you can delete all game data by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google or Apple, use their tools, or contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
