# BCM-M33-R01 Independent Audit V01

**Verdict: PARTIAL / BLOCKED for reachability qualification. Evidence framework delivered; requested physics verification NOT delivered.**

Independently reviewed published M33-R01 builder log, README and full coverage matrix summary on main. Matrix enumerates all 899 candidate levels (global 102–1000) but only 11 distinct candidates were sampled, with 13 candidate trials, zero completions, 11 NOT_COMPLETED_WITHIN_BUDGET and 888 NOT_RUN/ERROR. A separate global 101 control completed in 11 shots. 14 total trials logged 384 shots and 410.309 trial wall seconds. No candidate proved impossible, nor reachable. The matrix's 899 entries are inventory, not 899 executed validations.

**Critical design problem:** Trial limits of 30–60 shots and 15–30 wall seconds were drastically below reported median theoretical normal-order shot requirements of 100 (Tiki) to 816 (Final Island). Numerous trials only fired about 31 shots before the 30-second cap. Under those conditions no-completion is expected and cannot indicate campaign quality. Merely increasing caps and doing hundreds of lengthy physics trials would impose major owner-machine runtime/disk burden and still give weak human-solvability evidence.

**Technical evidence:** Python static candidate checks, Tiki control/VIP targeted probes, Godot script syntax parse, summarizer integrity and resumable checkpoint were builder-reported PASS. Full clean editor import stalled twice and was not proven. No generation, campaign or gameplay data fixes were justified, and none were made. Owner saved data preservation and isolated runner identity were documented; not independently repeated on user's machine.

**Decision:** Do not close M33-004 or claim 1000 playable levels. Accept R01 runner/matrix as instrumentation only. Before any mass trial, perform R02 feasibility and runtime budget calibration: validate shot-count arithmetic vs current 4T move limits, run a small stratified real-physics pilot with level-relative shot budgets, inspect solver/harness strategy limitations and estimate realistic run cost. Design early impossibility screening separately from stochastic agent failures. Set strict global computation/disk thresholds and owner checkpoint; if model cannot complete meaningful tests, report BLOCKED rather than running 899 blindly. Preserve owner progress and existing V05 accepted tutorial. Product publishing remains deferred.
