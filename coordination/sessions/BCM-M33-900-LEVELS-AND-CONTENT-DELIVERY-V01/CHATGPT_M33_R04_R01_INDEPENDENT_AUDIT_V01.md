# BCM-M33-R04-R01 independent audit V01

**Verdict: PASS for feasibility analysis and safe stop; content redesign remains BLOCKED_OWNER_DESIGN_DECISION_REQUIRED.**

Reviewed published Codex R01 V01 builder log, design-space/baseline evidence and R01 README. Under its enumerated existing recipe-schema restrictions (2–3 distinct To-Go levels, quantity 1–2, L6+ normal targets, ordinary T<=56 and listed optional VIP variants), there are at most 8 semantic normal sets, 20 order-sensitive normal signatures, 40 semantic full sets counting VIP variants and 100 order-sensitive full signatures. The 879 ordinary generated records cannot all have distinct full recipes: even the generous 100 maximum leaves 779 duplicate slots. L9 costs T64, so L9 cannot occur as an ordinary objective under T<=56. These figures are conditional on existing profile schema and enumeration; broadening the allowed gameplay variation changes the bound.

R04 baseline: 888 generated levels (879 ordinary plus nine capstones), 717 ordinary rows have L5 objective(s), all 879 ordinary T<=56, 361 cross-island duplicate full records, 539 unique full signatures for 900 packaged Tiki-to-Final records including inherited Tiki. README numerator 870/879 was corrected to 879/879. Codex did not regenerate contradictory L6+-only levels or alter gameplay, move/star/VIP rules, save IDs, 12 Tiki seed records or Sunny Cove. Focused tests and Godot content/navigation probe PASS as builder reported; independent audit is source/evidence review, not a replay of owner's local Godot installation.

**Design decision:** Prior GPT instruction requiring all 888 generated levels globally unique under L6+ and T56 was overly restrictive. Owner never explicitly chose strict global uniqueness, only a fun 1000-level campaign with short ordinary sessions. Choose between (A) retaining T<=56 and L6+ with **deliberate, measured repeats** compensated by island art, progression, objective ordering and tested play feel; (B) expanding mechanics / objective schema to create genuinely different challenges without excessive shots; or (C) widening session/difficulty envelope (must have explicit owner approval). Cosmetic VIP/order shuffling alone must not masquerade as meaningful variety. A new content regeneration prompt should be written only after owner confirms direction.

No production redesign is accepted yet, no mass physics trials, and no new Desktop root artifacts. Store release remains out of scope.
