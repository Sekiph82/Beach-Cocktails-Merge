# M32 milestone audit criteria

M32 is game development, not store release. Required: ten islands of exactly 100 levels under an explicit source contract; never claim nine islands complete when only one has content. M32-001 validates campaign schema and content-authoring pipeline, existing 100 Sunny Cove levels unchanged; missing entries are acknowledged as pending rather than silently filled with identical dummy levels. M32-002 delivers the FIRST next-island end-to-end vertical slice with playable tested content and working map/unlock, no dead ends; full 100 levels for that island may be staged only when quality/reachability is verified. M32-003 designs and implements usable free coin/gem/shop/booster baseline with real persistence, transactions/consumption, replay-safe grants and no fake displays; scope controls prevent accidental IAP/ads. M32-004 interactive real-game tutorial with skip/replay, save and mobile touch behavior. Separate child commits and logs, single master handoff and GPT audit. Preserve local owner files and original saves, no unapproved visual redesign, full regression only if changes justify it, root TASKS.md GPT only.
