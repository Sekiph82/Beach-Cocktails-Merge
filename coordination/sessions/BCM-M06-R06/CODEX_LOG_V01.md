## Implementation

`scripts/game_manager.gd` now represents the visible tabletop side boundary as two independent five-point source-space polylines (`TABLE_LEFT_EDGE_SOURCE_POINTS` and `TABLE_RIGHT_EDGE_SOURCE_POINTS`) sampled at source Y values 472, 620, 800, 1000 and 1186. `get_table_rail_bounds_at_y()` performs piecewise interpolation and clamps only to the visible viewport when source-cover cropping puts the visible wood beyond the screen edge.

The independent dataset is `docs/evidence/m06_r06/independent_table_edges.json`. It records source-space measurements and separately recorded viewport-space visible-edge observations for all required portrait viewports. It is not generated by calling production boundary helpers.

`_build_walls()` creates one physical segment per measured polyline interval and offsets each segment outward by half wall thickness. Horizontal center bounds continue to remove only physical radius plus `TABLE_SOLVER_EPSILON`; HUD nodes are not consulted. Danger Y (`1080` source) and launch Y (`1136` source), launch speed, deceleration, physics, merge, economy and persistence code were not changed.

`tests/m06_r06_full_tabletop_probe.gd` compares the production boundary against the independent five-sample dataset and renders actual L01/L06/L12 left/right contact nodes at rear/far through middle depths. `tests/m06_r06_geometry_overlay.gd` retains independent-edge, production-polyline and contact overlays.
