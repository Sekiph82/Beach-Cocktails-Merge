# Move-budget introduction locked criteria

1. A move means precisely one successful `shot_fired` / committed launched drink. Touch release, mouse-up, canceled/no-shot, UI click, pause and duplicate input cannot consume extra moves. Count after authoritative shot commit, once. A session has configured move_limit and moves_remaining; no time limit added.
2. Levels with move_limit defined enforce it. If remaining hits zero and required To-Go orders are incomplete after the final shot's merges/collections have finished resolving, emit ONE natural LOSE reason MOVES_EXHAUSTED, with immutable score and objectives; if last move completed objective, WIN takes precedence. Do not terminate immediately before shot settles. Deterministic terminal sequencing, retries reset budget; pause/resume and transitions keep correct state; avoid saving a half-state.
3. LV6 candidate 35 moves is a starting design figure, not proof of fairness. Run solver/Monte Carlo/automated legitimate launch trials and estimate success rate and ability to achieve stars; do not fabricate inputs or terminal results. To avoid accidentally making the other 99 levels impossible, do not bulk-enable arbitrary 35 on all. Add schema/default strategy with explicit fallback, provide per-level budget recommendation and owner review before global rollout.
4. Preserve current score-based star thresholds for this implementation. Separate non-applied design option: move-based performance tiers, e.g. Level 6 3 stars <=24, 2 stars <=30, 1 star <=35 successful shots when orders complete, subject to evidence/owner approval. VIP third-star gate must remain intact unless explicitly changed. Do not silently change existing rewards/progression.
5. HUD should show clear `MOVES 35`/remaining and warning when low without obscuring existing To-Go/VIP/table/shelf/Score/Next. Never fake or bake into master image. Match mobile 720x1280 and 720x1440, Reduced Effects. User feedback and before/after captures required.
6. Regression: natural WIN on final move vs LOSE after unsuccessful last move, Retry via real GUI pointer/touch, ordinary and VIP objective interplay, no extra shot when 0, one loss presentation no Spark, 1/2/3 stars existing scoring parity, M02/M09/M15/M21/M22/M23/M24/M25, GL mobile clean exit, import/boot.
