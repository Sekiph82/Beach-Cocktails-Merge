# BCM-M20-003 — Locked Criteria

- Separate settings schema/store exists.
- All six minimum settings are represented and persisted.
- Missing/corrupt settings recover to safe defaults.
- Campaign save is not modified merely by changing settings.
- Audio bus application is safe when expected buses are absent.
- Haptics are setting-gated.
- Reduced-motion/high-contrast state is exposed without gameplay-math changes.
- Focused probe PASS and separate publication/equality required.
