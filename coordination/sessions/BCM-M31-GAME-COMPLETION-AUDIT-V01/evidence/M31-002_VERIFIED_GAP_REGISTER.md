## Classification rules

- `VERIFIED_MISSING`: absent from current production source/data, confirmed directly from canonical files or prior independent source audit.
- `VERIFIED_PARTIAL`: a partial feature surface exists, but current code/data has no end-to-end product path for a claimed extension.
- `UNVERIFIED`: no reliable evidence proves the behavior/risk either way; not presented as a reproduced defect.
- `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`: required scope or policy is not defined by current owner-authoritative artifacts.

Priority labels: `P0` blocks declaring the game complete; `P1` required before completion once the owner establishes scope; `P2` product quality / risk closure; `PARKED` intentionally outside this game-completion milestone.
