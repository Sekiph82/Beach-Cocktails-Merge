- The table is a close, strongly perspective board filling almost the full screen width near the player.
- Its rear edge begins below the current locked HUD region.
- It extends deeply toward the player and continues much farther down the screen than V07-R03.
- The near/front edge is close to the player and nearly full screen width.
- No large empty sand gap may separate the tabletop from progression.
- Do not show long table legs merely to make the table physically complete.
- The L1-L12 progression must be a **wide horizontal strip integrated immediately under the player-facing table front**, following the master composition.
- The 12 cocktails must not be squeezed into a small panel between legs.
- The progression strip must feel like part of the front table assembly and use most of the available width.
- A bottom wooden instruction/control fascia may follow the master composition if it matches current gameplay interaction and does not introduce false controls.
